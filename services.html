Travel

Included within 20 miles of NG1. Beyond that, 45p per mile each way, quoted up front. Overnight stays for far-flung venues are arranged at cost.

Early starts

Ready-by times before 7am carry a £25 early-start fee. Most wedding mornings begin between 6am and 8am depending on party size.

Deposits

A £50 deposit secures a wedding date once we've spoken; the balance is due one week before. Occasion bookings are paid on the day.

Groups

Six or more faces on one morning brings an assistant artist so nobody is rushed. Group lessons are £55 per person, minimum three.

Three questions almost everyone asks. The rest live on the FAQ page.

Is the trial really included?

Yes — every bridal package includes a two-hour studio trial. If you'd like a second trial (a different look, or a new dress), it's £60.

What does a bridal party of six cost?

Bride £180 plus five at £65 — £505 in total, with an assistant artist included so the morning runs to time. Lashes are £15 per person if wanted.

Can I hold a date before paying a deposit?

Dates are pencilled for seven days after your quote, free of charge. After that the £50 deposit confirms it.
